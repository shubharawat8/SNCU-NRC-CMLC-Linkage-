**In one line:** Checks feeding, weight gain and illness, and spots infants who need more help.

## Role (guideline Section 11)
Assess feeding, weight gain and illness; identify infants requiring further intervention. Medical Officer/Paediatrician makes the clinical assessment and decision regarding referral/escalation.

## What the guideline asks
- Consider referral for: feeding difficulty with poor weight gain; SAM; persistent feeding problem despite breastfeeding support.
- Assessment by ANM/Medical Officer/Paediatrician, as applicable, then nutritional and clinical assessment before any NRC referral.

## Receives from
- SNCU graduates
- Community (ASHA · ANM · AWW)

## Sends on to
- NRC / appropriate facility (if criteria met)
- Breastfeeding Counsellor
- Community follow-up

## Monitors (Section 13)
- SNCU graduates attending follow-up
- Infants assessed for feeding
- Infants with poor/no weight gain
- Infants with feeding difficulty
- Infants identified with SAM
- Infants referred to NRC
- Infants completing referral

## Records (Section 14)
- SNCU follow-up record

## Practice notes (Supplementary, not guideline text)
- Plot weight at every visit — a flat line is the earliest signal of growth faltering.
