**In one line:** Manages eligible children with SAM or nutritional complications — when admission criteria are met.

## Role (guideline Section 11)
Management of eligible children with SAM/nutritional complications as per protocol, including breastfeeding/nutrition support.

## What the guideline asks
- NRC referral should follow applicable nutritional and clinical admission criteria.
- For children with SAM, NRC admission and management should follow applicable state/NHM criteria and protocols.
- Every referral should have a clear counter-referral and community follow-up plan.
